About Us

We Bring People Together

Peace and Security Bridges is dedicated to humanitarian response operations and sustainable peacebuilding. We work with communities, governments and civil society to transform conflict environments into spaces of stability, hope and shared humanity.

Our work promotes peace, security, humanitarian access, and sustainable coexistence in high-risk and conflict-affected regions of Africa, through dialogue, humanitarian response and emergency support.

Where dialogue has broken down and communities are divided, we help rebuild the connections that allow people to live together in safety and dignity.

The Consultation

How a PSB Consultation Works

A consultation is a structured conversation held in writing, open for a limited time, around one question that matters to the communities concerned.

1

A Defined Question Opens

A consultation begins when a defined question is opened for a time-bound round, so every participant knows exactly what the conversation is about and how long it will run.

2

Contributions in Writing, From Anywhere

Participants contribute in writing, in English or French, from wherever they are. The format is text-based and asynchronous, and stays light enough for low-bandwidth settings.

3

Shared Findings and Next Steps

When the round closes, contributions are synthesised into shared findings and next steps that communities and partners can act on together.

Why Dialogue Works

Built for the Places Where Peace Is Made

Our consultations are designed for the realities of conflict-affected regions: distance, fragile connections, two working languages, and the need to speak safely.

Dialogue Without Borders

Focused Discussions

Each round is time-bound and centred on one defined question, organised into several threads, so practitioners, humanitarians and community voices can share knowledge where it counts.

Safe and Protected

Consultations are held under the Chatham House rule: what is said can inform the work, but it is never attributed. Where safety requires it, participants may contribute anonymously.

Peace Dialogues

Consultations in Dialogue

Peace and Security Bridges runs dialogue consultations under its Peace Dialogues program, bringing communities in conflict, and the people who work alongside them, into one protected conversation.

Consultation rounds are announced here and on our LinkedIn page as they are opened. If your community or organisation faces a question that a structured dialogue could help answer, we invite you to propose it.
